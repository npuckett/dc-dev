the system has been running for several weeks under the notion of attempting to attract people from the passive zone into the active. For various reasons outside the scope of this project, that hasn't worked. The vast majority of input to the system are people walking by on the very busy sidewalk.

- I don't want to completely remake the behavior system, but I do want to re-tune it with different pritorities

1. Focus on being more directly affected and engaged with the passive movement amounts and directions. This is the largest dataset, so it should be the focus of the digital character's movements. Look heavily at the previous trends and see how it can be dynamic, but within the given ranges. It should have different personalities based on time of day etc

2. Still have special aknowledgement and gesture for people with the active zone.
