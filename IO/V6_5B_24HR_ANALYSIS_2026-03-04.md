## 11. Recommendations for Further Improvement

### HIGH PRIORITY

#### 1. Re-Enable Systemd Service
The controller is currently running as a manual process (PID 2087). If the machine reboots or the process is killed, the installation goes dark. Re-enable `light-controller.service` so it auto-starts and auto-restarts on failure. The service was stopped at 11:52 on March 3 and never restarted via systemd.

#### 2. Investigate the "Unknown" Zone (48.5% of Events)
Nearly half of all tracking events are classified as `zone: unknown`. This suggests the zone boundary definitions don't cover the full camera field of view. Review and expand the zone polygon definitions in the camera calibration to capture more of the visible area — even if it's all classified as passive, it would give cleaner data about where people actually are.

#### 3. Cap Crowd-Mode Brightness at ~600
The V6.5b max brightness hit 1,035 at 19:00 during a brief crowd spike. While massively better than the 34,914 pre-V6.5b maxes, consider adding a hard ceiling at ~600 to prevent any outlier spikes. A single `min(brightness, 600)` clamp in the crowd mode path would suffice.

### MEDIUM PRIORITY

#### 4. Tune the Almost-Engaged Pipeline
The almost-engaged pathway had **0 triggers** in 24h. This means either: (a) the threshold for "almost-engaged" is too high, so no one qualifies, or (b) the passive tiers are sufficient and this path is genuinely unnecessary. Worth testing with a lower threshold to see if the bandit strategy can nudge borderline visitors into engagement. If it remains at 0 after testing, consider removing it to simplify the code.

#### 5. Add Bloom Tracking to hourly_stats
The `bloom_count` column in `hourly_stats` is recording 0 for every hour despite 248 bloom gestures in the `light_behavior` table. The bloom counter isn't being wired to the hourly stats aggregation — a simple fix in the stats writer.

#### 6. Explore Time-of-Day Bandit Context Granularity
The bandit uses 4 time periods (morning, afternoon, evening, night). The data shows distinct behavioral signatures at finer resolution — e.g., 08:00 rush vs 10:00 mid-morning are quite different. Consider splitting morning into `early_morning` (06-09) and `late_morning` (09-12) to let the bandit learn more nuanced strategies.

#### 7. Idle-Mode Win Rate Improvement
`idle_night` has the lowest win rate at ~60% (vs 90%+ for active periods). This makes sense since engagement signal is weak during idle, but the bandit is spending 28% of all its exploration budget there (1,537 of 5,470 pulls). Consider reducing exploration in idle contexts or using a different fitness signal for idle periods (e.g., aesthetic quality rather than engagement).

### LOW PRIORITY

#### 8. Persist Engagement Feedback to Database
The engagement feedback log is a flat text file that grows indefinitely and is hard to query. Consider logging engagement events to a new SQLite table (`engagement_events`) with structured columns for mode, duration, aggression, zone, strategy, and timestamp. This would enable much richer longitudinal analysis.

#### 9. Average Speed Anomaly
Daily avg speed jumped from ~1,000–3,000 in late February to 9,000–11,000 on March 1–2, then back to 1,471 on March 3 (V6.5b). The speed calculation may have been affected by a tracking or coordinate system issue in the older version. Worth monitoring to confirm V6.5b's speed values are stable.

#### 10. Position Entropy
The daily report shows position entropy at 0.139 — quite low, meaning the light tends to hover in a limited area. Consider slightly increasing wander velocity or wander box size during idle/flow to create more spatial variety and visual interest for passers-by who see it from a distance.

#### 11. Generate Automated Nightly Analysis
The daily report system (`daily-reports.service`) is generating basic JSON reports. Consider adding a script that runs this kind of mode/brightness/gesture analysis automatically each night and appends it to a running log or dashboard page, reducing the need for manual analysis sessions.
