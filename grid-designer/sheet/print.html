Front bar fouls on concave jointsA flat bar clears the bezels up to 12.4° of concave fold. This design folds 20°, so the 30 concave connectors (P01) need a different bar section. The overlap shows in the P01 section above.
Pieces rounded to the connector binEvery joint here is 22 mm, but the kit rounds gaps to 5 mm steps. The back halves are built for 20 mm and the bar is 40 mm wide instead of 42. Set the editor's span bin to 0.20 cm or finer to build them at exactly 22 mm.
Holes not modelledThe printable parts are solid. Bolt clearance holes and insert bores still need to be cut, and the three bolt positions along the part are not fixed yet.
